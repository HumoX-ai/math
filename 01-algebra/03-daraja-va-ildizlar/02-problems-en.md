## B. Power rules

## C. Roots

**C1** ⭐  `√49`, `√0.25`, `³√(−8)`, `⁴√81`, `√(16/9)`, `³√(1/27)`, `⁵√(−32)`

**C2** ⭐⭐  `√((−5)²)`; `√(x²)` at `x = −3`; `³√((−5)³)`
> One of the three behaves differently. Which, and why?

**C3** ⭐⭐  Simplify: `√50`, `√72`, `√200`, `³√54`, `√12 + √27`

**C4** ⭐⭐  `√2·√8`; `√3·√12`; `√50/√2`; `³√4·³√16`

**C5** ⭐⭐⭐  Rationalize the denominator: `1/√2`; `3/√12`; `1/(√3 − 1)`; `2/(√5 + √3)`

**C6** ⭐⭐  `(√5 + √3)²`; `(√7 − √2)(√7 + √2)`; `(2√3)²`

**C7** ⭐⭐  With `a = 9`, `b = 16` compute `√(a + b)` and `√a + √b`. Equal?
**When** is `√(a + b) = √a + √b` true? (Square both sides.)

**C8** ⭐⭐⭐  Simplify `√(x² − 6x + 9)`. Evaluate at `x = 1` and `x = 5`. Write it piecewise.

## D. Rational exponents

**D1** ⭐⭐  `8^(1/3)`, `16^(1/4)`, `27^(2/3)`, `4^(3/2)`, `32^(−1/5)`, `81^(−3/4)`
> Order: minus → flip; denominator → root; numerator → power.

**D2** ⭐  Write as powers: `√x`, `³√(x²)`, `1/√x`, `⁴√(x⁵)`, `(√x)³`

**D3** ⭐⭐  Simplify (`x > 0`): `x^(1/2)·x^(1/3)`; `x^(3/4)/x^(1/4)`; `(x^(2/3))^(3/2)`; `(x⁶)^(1/3)`

**D4** ⭐⭐⭐  Write `√x · ³√x` as a single root. Write `√(x√x)` as a power.

**D5** ⭐⭐  `(0.001)^(1/3)`; `(1/16)^(−1/2)`; `0.25^(−1/2)`; `(8/27)^(2/3)`

**D6** ⭐⭐⭐  **Prove:** if the power rules hold for fractional exponents, then
`a^(1/2) = √a` is **forced**. Then derive `a^(m/n) = (ⁿ√a)ᵐ`.

(b) Raw scores `[64, 16, 0]`, `d = 64`. After `÷√d`?
(c) Why `√d` and not `d`? In your own words (hint: a sum of `n` random ±1's is typically

### G4 ⭐⭐ — RMS and RMSNorm

(a) `RMS(x) = √(Σxᵢ²/n)`.
(b) `x' = x / RMS(x)`. Compute it.
(c) `RMS(x') = ?` Why is that the point?

### G5 ⭐⭐⭐ — Learning-rate schedule

(a) `η(t) = η₀/√t`, `η₀ = 0.1`. `η` at `t = 1, 4, 100, 10 000`.
(b) When is `η < 0.001`? (Solve the inequality.)
(c) Transformer: `η = d^(−0.5) · min(t^(−0.5), t·w^(−1.5))`, `d = 512`, `w = 4000`.
`η` at `t = w`? (This is the maximum — the "peak lr".) Take `√2 048 000 ≈ 1431`.
(d) For `t < w`, does `η` increase or decrease? For `t > w`?
