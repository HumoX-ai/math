## D. Ratsional ko'rsatkich

**D1** ⭐⭐  `8^(1/3)`, `16^(1/4)`, `27^(2/3)`, `4^(3/2)`, `32^(−1/5)`, `81^(−3/4)`
> Tartib: minus → ag'dar; maxraj → ildiz; surat → daraja.

**D2** ⭐  Daraja ko'rinishida yoz: `√x`, `³√(x²)`, `1/√x`, `⁴√(x⁵)`, `(√x)³`

**D3** ⭐⭐  Soddalashtir (`x > 0`): `x^(1/2)·x^(1/3)`; `x^(3/4)/x^(1/4)`; `(x^(2/3))^(3/2)`; `(x⁶)^(1/3)`

**D4** ⭐⭐⭐  `√x · ³√x` ni bitta ildiz ko'rinishida yoz. `√(x√x)` ni daraja ko'rinishida yoz.

**D5** ⭐⭐  `(0.001)^(1/3)`; `(1/16)^(−1/2)`; `0.25^(−1/2)`; `(8/27)^(2/3)`

**D6** ⭐⭐⭐  **Isbotla:** agar daraja qoidalari kasr ko'rsatkichlar uchun ham ishlasa,
`a^(1/2) = √a` bo'lishi **majburiy**. Keyin `a^(m/n) = (ⁿ√a)ᵐ` ni chiqar.

## G. 🔗 AI

### G1 ⭐⭐ — L2 norm

(a) `‖(3, 4)‖`, `‖(1, 2, 2)‖`, `‖(1, 1, 1, 1)‖`
(b) `(3, 4)` ning birlik vektori. Normasi 1 ekanini tekshir.
(c) `n` ta `1` dan iborat vektor normasi? `n = 64`, `n = 512` uchun.
> (c) — `1/√d` ning kelib chiqish joyi.

### G2 ⭐⭐⭐ — Attention scaling `1/√d_k`

(a) `d = 64, 128, 256, 512, 4096` uchun `√d` va `1/√d`.
(b) Xom ballar `[64, 16, 0]`, `d = 64`. `÷√d` dan keyin?
(c) Nima uchun `√d`, `d` emas? O'z so'zing bilan (ishora: `n` ta ±1 ning yig'indisi
tipik jihatdan `√n` ga yaqin, `n` ga emas — `code/03_ai_bogliqlik.py` da tekshir).

### G3 ⭐⭐ — He initialization `√(2/n)`

(a) `n_in = 512, 128, 2048, 8` uchun `std = √(2/n_in)`.
(b) Qatlam kengroq → std kichikroq. Nega mantiqan?
(c) `std = 0.05` bo'lishi uchun `n_in = ?`

### G4 ⭐⭐ — RMS va RMSNorm

`x = (1, −2, 2, 4)`.
(a) `RMS(x) = √(Σxᵢ²/n)`.
(b) `x' = x / RMS(x)`. Hisobla.
(c) `RMS(x') = ?` Bu nima uchun kerak?

### G5 ⭐⭐⭐ — Learning rate schedule

(a) `η(t) = η₀/√t`, `η₀ = 0.1`. `t = 1, 4, 100, 10 000` da `η`.
(b) `η < 0.001` qachon bo'ladi? (Tengsizlik yech.)
(c) Transformer: `η = d^(−0.5) · min(t^(−0.5), t·w^(−1.5))`, `d = 512`, `w = 4000`.
`t = w` da `η = ?` (Bu — eng katta qiymat, "peak lr".) `√2 048 000 ≈ 1431` deb ol.
(d) `t < w` da `η` ortadimi yoki kamayadimi? `t > w` da?
